# Browse, Phase 2c: amendment 1 (money only, after the pilot, before the counted sessions)

Written 2026-09-30 after Phase 1c confirmed (R@20 89.4 %, Wilson 83.3-93.5 %) and after the
registered Phase 2c pilot (3 steps per split, excluded), before any counted session.

The pilot measured what a FULL session costs: 0.167 USD at list price per step (35,257 input
tokens on average), TOP 0.011 USD. The 60 registered steps then need about 10.7 USD including the
pilot's 1.60, past the registered 8.00 USD ceiling, which would stop the run part-way and leave
it unreadable.

Changed: the Phase 2c ceiling is 12.00 USD. Unchanged: steps, arms, prompt, schema, metric,
decision rule, the per-session cap. The pilot's accuracies (FULL 6/9, TOP 5/9) are not part of
any count, as registered; they are reported apart.
